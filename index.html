01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

India is not one audience.
Know who you're reaching.

A game that finds an audience in one market will not automatically find the same response in another. Player preferences, platforms, price expectations, languages and discovery habits all matter.

We help studios replace assumptions with market understanding — then use focused tests to learn how Indian players respond to a specific game.
